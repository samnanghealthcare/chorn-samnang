- audio.wav : the user-supplied narration (uploaded as audio.mp3; the file is actually WAV/PCM 24 kHz mono). Used unedited except a 70 Hz high-pass and loudness normalisation.

== TECHNICAL ILLUSTRATIONS ==
- All blueprints, diagrams, icons and the exploded jack view are drawn in code (source/gfx.py, source/scenes.py).
  Aircraft outlines are a GENERIC narrow-body twinjet, labelled 'illustrative / not to scale'. Jacking-point positions are
  illustrative (nose + one per wing, as the narration states); exact locations and loads must be taken from the aircraft
  manufacturer's AMM, chapter ATA 07 (Lifting & Shoring).
