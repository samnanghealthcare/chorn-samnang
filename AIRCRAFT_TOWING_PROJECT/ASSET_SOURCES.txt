AIRCRAFT GENERAL PRACTICES – AIRCRAFT TOWING
Asset sources and licences
=====================================================================

CURRENT RENDER (output/AIRCRAFT_TOWING_DOCUMENTARY.mp4)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
No third-party photos or video clips are used in the current render.
Wikimedia Commons (the only free-media site reachable from the build
environment) refused downloads with HTTP 429 "robot policy", so every
photo/video slot falls back to an original schematic illustration
(src/graphics/Illustrated.tsx). The video contains no third-party
imagery.

ORIGINAL CONTENT (created for this project)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
- All motion graphics, technical illustrations, airport map, A320
  schematic, tug/towbar/nose-gear diagram, turbofan X-ray / exploded
  view, icons, cycle, iceberg and timeline: original SVG/CSS code in src/.
- Background music and all sound effects: synthesised procedurally by
  tools/generate_audio.py (numpy). No samples or third-party audio.
- Narration: audio.source.wav, supplied by the project owner (unmodified,
  only resampled to 48 kHz for the mix).

FACTS SHOWN ON SCREEN
---------------------------------------------------------------------
- A320 overall length 37.57 m, height 11.76 m:
  Airbus, "A320 Aircraft Characteristics – Airport and Maintenance Planning".
- ATA chapter 09 = Towing & Taxiing (ATA iSpec 2200 chapter numbering).
- Airport map: generic schematic layout, not any real airport.
- Turbofan: generic high-bypass illustration, not a specific engine model.
